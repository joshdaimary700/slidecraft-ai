3. Phone Screenshots (Min 2 required)

To capture 1080x2400 high-res screenshots from Chrome in 30 seconds:

  1. Open your SlideCraft AI app in Chrome (e.g. http://localhost:5173).
  2. Press F12 to open Developer Tools.
  3. Click Toggle Device Toolbar (Ctrl + Shift + M).
  4. Set Dimensions to 1080 x 2400 (or select Pixel 7).
  5. Press Ctrl + Shift + P in DevTools, type "Capture full size screenshot", and press Enter!
  6. Take screenshots of:
    • Home / Landing Screen
    • Generator Workspace (AI topic input)
    • Slide Editor & Preview
    • Export / Admin Dashboard
